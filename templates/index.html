How to Get a Gmail App Password

Required by Google for secure automated email dispatch

1
Enable 2-Step Verification: Go to your Google Account security settings and make sure 2-Step Verification is turned ON.
2
Open App Passwords Page: Directly open:
https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords →
3
Generate Code: In the App name box, type Job Mailer and click Create. Google will show you a yellow banner with a 16-letter password (e.g. abcd efgh ijkl mnop).
4
Paste into AutoReach: Copy and paste that 16-letter code into the AutoReach password field. You're done!
